FPC Drum Mapper & Humanizer
FPC_Drum_Mapper_Humanizer.pyscript
Automatically maps separated kick, snare, and hi-hat triggers onto FPC pads (Kick=C3, Snare=E3, Closed Hat=F#3) with dynamic velocity scaling and backbeat pocket lay-back (+4 ticks).
Groove Humanizer
Groove_Humanize.pyscript
Injects natural micro-timing shifts, swing push/pull, and dynamic velocity randomization across bass or percussion to eliminate machine stiffness.
Stem MIDI Cleaner & Pad Mapper
Stem_MIDI_Cleaner.pyscript
Cleans audio-to-MIDI transcription artifacts, de-overlaps simultaneous note collisions, removes ghost triggers, and tightens rhythm to grid anchors.
Humanized Mix: drums & bass re-performed from MIDI

Drums are re-triggered from hits sampled out of this song's own drum stem, and bass is played by a synth. Both follow the transcribed MIDI with a human timing & velocity model: shared band drift, per-player drift and jitter, snare/bass lay-back, hats pushed slightly ahead, optional swing. Each take (seed) is a different performance. If you edit the *_FPC_Drums.mid / *_Bass.mid files in FL Studio and save them back, the next render follows your edit.
